# Däm image attribution

## Supplied menu photographs

The ten JPG files are copied unchanged from the project’s `Photo/` folder.
See README.md for the exact filename-to-dish mapping and credits.json for provenance.
The user confirmed matching by filename even where pictured contents, portions,
or advertised prices differ from menu data. No public license is asserted.
The logo at `/static/images/logo.jpg` is copied unchanged from `Photo/Logo.jpg`.
